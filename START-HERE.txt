1. Extract this website ZIP into a new folder named NMD-WEBSITE.
2. Extract your original fountainn.zip.
3. Copy the entire 01-Fountain folder into NMD-WEBSITE, next to index.html.
4. Keep all original folder and media filenames unchanged.
5. Open index.html to check the website, then upload the whole NMD-WEBSITE folder to your existing Netlify project.

These website files reference your original PNG photos and MP4 videos directly. No media is resized, compressed or re-encoded.
Jumbo Silver and Super Jumbo Silver have no supplied media.
